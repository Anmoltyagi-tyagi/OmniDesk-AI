# CONTOSO ENTERPRISE — ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT

> **Synthetic content notice.** Contoso Enterprise is a fictional organization created for a hackathon prototype. Nothing in these documents describes real Microsoft or Contoso policies. All names, amounts, extensions, URLs and processes are invented.

## 1. Organization

| Item | Convention |
|---|---|
| Organization name | Contoso Enterprise |
| Currency | All amounts are in USD unless stated otherwise |
| Fiscal year | Calendar year (1 January – 31 December) |
| Email domain | contoso.example |
| Internal service portal | **Contoso ServiceHub** (https://servicehub.contoso.example), referred to as "ServiceHub" |
| Sign-in identity | **Contoso Account** (first.last@contoso.example) |
| Employee identifier | **Employee ID**, format CE-12345 |

## 2. Terminology

- **Employee**: a permanent or fixed-term member of staff on the Contoso Enterprise payroll.
- **Contractor**: an external worker engaged under a services contract. Contractors are not employees and only receive the entitlements their contract states.
- **Manager**: an employee's direct line manager as recorded in the Contoso Directory.
- **Department head**: the senior leader of a business department (Engineering, Sales, Operations, Marketing, Legal & Compliance, or a support department).
- **Cost center**: the budget code of a department, used to charge approved costs.
- **Working day**: Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays on the Holiday Calendar for the employee's office.
- **Probation period**: the first 3 months of employment for new employees.

## 3. Departments

Business departments: Engineering, Sales, Operations, Marketing, Legal & Compliance.
Support departments (owners of the four knowledge bases): **Information Technology (IT)**, **Human Resources (HR)**, **Finance**, **Facilities & Workplace Services (Facilities)**.

## 4. Offices

- **HQ Campus**: Building A (floors 1–6; IT, Finance, Facilities service desk, Engineering) and Building B (floors 1–4; HR, Sales, Operations, Marketing). Each building has a ground-floor reception. The Security desk is at Building A reception.
- **Regional Office East** and **Regional Office West**: single-building offices, each with a Site Coordinator who assists with local logistics.
- Room codes use building letter, floor and number (for example, A3-04 is Building A, floor 3, room 04).

## 5. Working Hours

- Standard working week: Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00 local office time, including a 1-hour break (8 working hours per day, 40 per week).
- Core hours: 10:00–16:00. Employees must be available during core hours.
- Buildings are open Monday–Friday 07:00–20:00. The Security desk operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## 7. Ticket Priorities (shared across all domains)

| Priority | Meaning | Initial response target |
|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | Safety risk, security incident, or outage affecting many people | 30 minutes |
| P2 High | One person or team cannot work and has no workaround | 2 working hours |
| P3 Normal | Degraded service or issue with a workaround | 1 working day |
| P4 Low | Information requests, minor or cosmetic issues | 2 working days |

Each domain defines its own resolution targets.

## 8. Policy and Version Conventions

- Document IDs: IT-KB-001, HR-KB-001, FIN-KB-001, FAC-KB-001.
- Versions use major.minor (1.0, 1.1, 2.0). All four documents are version 1.0, effective 2026-10-01, reviewed annually.
- Sections are numbered (for example, Section 5.2). References between documents use the document ID and section number.
- "Must" is mandatory, "should" is strongly recommended, "may" is optional.
