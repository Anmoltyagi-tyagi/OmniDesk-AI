# HR Knowledge Base — Employment, Leave and Benefits

## 1. Purpose

This document explains the employment policies and HR processes that apply to employees of Contoso Enterprise: leave, work from home, attendance, benefits, personal information, onboarding and offboarding. It tells employees what they are entitled to, how to apply, and where to go when something does not work as expected.

## 2. Scope

This document applies to permanent and fixed-term employees at the HQ Campus, Regional Office East and Regional Office West. Contractors are covered only where Section 4.1 or Section 7.4 says so. Technology provisioning is described in IT-KB-001, expense and reimbursement rules in FIN-KB-001, and building access in FAC-KB-001. HR processes in this document often trigger work in those domains, and the relevant steps are noted where they occur.

## 3. General Policy

### 3.1 Annual Leave

Employees earn annual leave from their start date. Entitlement per calendar year depends on completed service:

| Completed service | Annual leave per year |
|---|---|
| Less than 3 years | 20 working days |
| 3 to under 7 years | 22 working days |
| 7 years or more | 25 working days |

Leave accrues monthly. For employees who join or leave during the year, entitlement is pro-rated by completed months and rounded up to the nearest half day. Public holidays on the Holiday Calendar for the employee's office are given in addition to annual leave.

Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year and must be used by 31 March; any other unused days are forfeited. Annual leave is not paid out except for up to 10 accrued unused days at employment end. Leave of more than 15 consecutive working days needs HR approval (Section 7.1).

Notice required: 1–2 days, at least 2 working days; 3–5 days, at least 5 working days; more than 5 days, at least 15 working days. Managers should respond within 2 working days.

### 3.2 Sick Leave and Work-Related Injury

Each employee has 10 paid sick days per calendar year, available from the start date and pro-rated for mid-year joiners. Sick days do not carry over.

- The employee must tell their manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.
- The absence must be recorded in ServiceHub within 2 working days of the first day.
- A medical certificate is required for absences of 3 or more consecutive working days (an absence of 1 or 2 days does not need one).
- When absence exceeds 5 consecutive working days, HR is notified and contacts the employee.
- After the 10 paid days are used, HR reviews the case. Unpaid medical leave of up to 12 weeks may be granted with medical documentation, followed by a fitness-to-return confirmation.

Absence caused by a work-related injury or illness is recorded as Work Injury Leave and does not reduce the 10-day sick entitlement. A Facilities incident report must exist for the injury (FAC-KB-001 Section 9.5).

### 3.3 Parental, Family and Bereavement Leave

| Leave type | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Birth parent | 16 weeks at full pay; may begin up to 2 weeks before the due date |
| Secondary caregiver (partner) | 4 weeks at full pay |
| Adoption or surrogacy, primary caregiver | 12 weeks at full pay |
| Adoption or surrogacy, secondary caregiver | 4 weeks at full pay |
| Family care leave | Up to 5 unpaid working days per year |
| Bereavement, immediate family (spouse, partner, child, parent, sibling) | 5 working days paid |
| Bereavement, other relatives (grandparent, parent-in-law) | 2 working days paid |

Parental leave requires 6 months of continuous service on the expected start date. Employees should notify HR at least 8 weeks before the expected date, or as soon as possible. Leave must be completed within 12 months of the birth or placement. Secondary caregiver leave may be taken in two blocks. Benefits continue during paid parental leave, and annual leave continues to accrue. On return, an employee may use a phased return of up to 4 weeks at 80% of working hours on full pay, agreed with the manager.

### 3.4 Work From Home and Remote Work

Contoso Enterprise operates a hybrid model. Eligible employees may work from home up to 2 days per week on days agreed with their manager.

- Employees in probation may work from home up to 1 day per week with manager approval.
- Roles designated as on-site by the department head (for example reception and facilities roles) are not eligible.
- Remote work must be done from the approved address in the employee's HR profile, within the country of the employee's office.
- Employees must be available during core hours (10:00–16:00) and must use a managed laptop. VPN is required for internal systems (IT-KB-001 Section 5.5).
- Extra remote days beyond 2 per week are at manager discretion, up to 5 per quarter.
- Fully remote roles are designated by HR and do not have a weekly office requirement.

**Home office support.** (a) A one-time home office stipend of USD 300 is available to employees whose standing hybrid arrangement is approved after probation. It covers furniture and non-IT items such as a chair, desk or lamp. Employees claim it as "Home Office Setup" within 60 days of HR approval (FIN-KB-001 Section 5.11). (b) IT lends a Remote Work Equipment Kit (monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset, laptop stand) (IT-KB-001 Section 5.10). (c) Fully remote employees receive a USD 25 monthly connectivity allowance through payroll.

### 3.5 Working Hours and Attendance

Standard hours are 09:00–18:00 with a 1-hour break. Core hours are 10:00–16:00. Employees may start between 08:00 and 10:00 and finish accordingly.

- Office attendance is recorded by badge swipe.
- On remote days, employees must check in through ServiceHub by 10:00.
- An arrival more than 30 minutes after the start of core hours without notice is recorded as unexplained absence until the manager confirms the reason.
- Salaried employees are not paid overtime. Work on a weekend or public holiday that the manager approved in advance earns a compensatory day off: 4 or more hours earns a half day and 8 or more hours a full day. Compensatory days must be taken within 60 days.
- Business travel on weekdays counts as working time and no leave is deducted. Required business travel on a weekend or public holiday of 4 or more hours earns compensatory time under the same rule.

### 3.6 Employee Benefits

| Life insurance | Automatic cover of 2 times annual base salary for permanent employees |

### 3.7 Employee Records and Confidentiality

HR keeps one employee record per person. Access is limited to HR and the employee's management chain on a need-to-know basis. Employees can view and update their own details in ServiceHub (Section 5.5). Payroll is paid on the last working day of each month; the cutoff for changes is the 20th.

## 4. Eligibility and Applicability

### 4.1 Employment Categories

Permanent and fixed-term employees follow this document. Contractors are engaged under contract and their entitlements are in the contract. Contractors do not receive annual leave, sick leave, benefits or the home office stipend. A contractor's sponsoring manager, not HR, requests accounts and access for them.

### 4.2 Probation Period Restrictions

Probation lasts 3 months. During probation: annual leave accrues and may be taken with manager approval; sick leave applies; work from home is limited to 1 day per week; the retirement match, the home office stipend and parental leave are not available until the relevant eligibility date.

### 4.3 Entitlement Summary

| Entitlement | Eligible from |
|---|---|
| Annual leave | Start date (accrues monthly) |
| Sick leave | Start date |
| Hybrid work (2 days/week) | After probation |
| Home office stipend | After probation, with approved standing arrangement |
| Retirement match | After probation |
| Parental leave | 6 months continuous service |
| Health insurance | Start date, if enrolled within 30 days |

## 5. Procedures

### 5.1 Applying for Annual Leave

1. In ServiceHub choose HR > Leave > New Request. Select dates and leave type.
2. Check the notice period (Section 3.1) and your balance.
3. Submit. The manager decides within 2 working days. If no decision by the third working day the manager is reminded, and on the fifth the request goes to the department head.
4. Approved leave appears in the team calendar. To cancel, withdraw the request in ServiceHub at least 1 working day before the first day.

### 5.2 Reporting Sick Leave

1. Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day (message or call).
2. Within 2 working days, create a Sick Leave request in ServiceHub.
3. For 3 or more consecutive working days, upload the medical certificate. On return, confirm the dates in ServiceHub.
4. If the absence passes 5 working days, HR contacts you to discuss support and return-to-work.

### 5.3 Requesting a Work From Home Arrangement

1. In ServiceHub choose HR > Work Arrangement and select Standing Hybrid (fixed days) or Ad hoc day.
2. Standing arrangements: propose fixed days and your approved address. The manager approves within 2 working days and HR records it. Arrangements are reviewed every 6 months.
3. Ad hoc remote day: obtain the manager's agreement and check in on ServiceHub by 10:00 that day.
4. After approval of a standing arrangement, request the equipment kit from IT and claim the stipend through Finance (Section 3.4).

### 5.4 Applying for Parental or Family Leave

1. Notify HR through ServiceHub (HR > Leave > Parental) at least 8 weeks before the expected start, with expected date and chosen leave type.
2. HR confirms eligibility (6 months of continuous service) and the leave dates within 5 working days.
3. HR informs the manager and arranges payroll and benefits continuity. You may request a phased return plan 4 weeks before returning.
4. For family care and bereavement leave, submit a Leave request and notify the manager. Bereavement leave needs no advance notice.

### 5.5 Updating Personal and Employment Information

| Change | How | Processing |
|---|---|---|
| Phone, personal email, home address, emergency contact, preferred name | Self-service in ServiceHub > HR > My Profile | Immediate |
| Bank account for payroll and reimbursements | Self-service entry plus bank proof; HR payroll verifies | 3 working days; effective for the next payroll if verified by the 20th |
| Legal name, marital status, dependents, tax details | HR ticket with supporting documents | 5 working days |
| Job title, department, manager, grade | Initiated by manager through HR | HR confirms effective date |

A change of home address is reflected in equipment delivery (IT-KB-001 Section 5.10) and remote work approval. Expense reimbursements are paid to the bank account in the HR profile (FIN-KB-001 Section 5.7).

### 5.6 Enrolling in Employee Benefits

1. In ServiceHub choose HR > Benefits and select health plan and dependents.
2. Complete enrollment within 30 days of joining. A qualifying life event (marriage, birth, adoption, loss of other coverage) opens a new 30-day window.
3. Upload dependent documents if requested. Coverage is effective from the start date if you enroll in time.

### 5.7 Correcting Attendance Records

If a badge swipe was missed, a remote check-in was forgotten, or an absence was marked wrongly, submit an Attendance Correction in ServiceHub (HR > Attendance) within 3 working days of the date. Provide the date, the working pattern and the reason. The manager approves, and HR updates the record. Corrections after the payroll cutoff on the 20th take effect in the following month.

### 5.8 Requesting an Office Transfer or Relocation

1. The employee or manager submits a Relocation / Transfer Request in ServiceHub (HR) at least 30 days before the intended move, stating the new office and whether the move is company-initiated or employee-initiated.
2. HR approves, sets the effective date, and issues an approval reference.
3. HR approval triggers tasks for Facilities (badge access for the new site, desk assignment), IT (device delivery and address update), and Finance (relocation allowance, for company-initiated moves only; see FIN-KB-001 Section 5.11). Payroll location data is updated by HR.

If the home address changes to a city too far from the assigned office to commute, the employee must submit a transfer or work arrangement request rather than only updating the address.

### 5.9 Onboarding a New Employee

1. **Pre-boarding:** after the offer is signed, HR creates the employee record and Employee ID. The new employee submits government ID, bank details, emergency contact and tax forms through a secure ServiceHub form at least 5 working days before the start date.
2. **Onboarding request:** at least 5 working days before the start date, the HR Onboarding Coordinator submits the onboarding request. This triggers IT (account and laptop, IT-KB-001 Section 6.1) and Facilities (badge, desk, locker, FAC-KB-001 Section 6.1).
3. **Day 1:** arrive at Building A reception at 09:30 and collect the badge from the Security desk with government ID. HR orientation is at 10:00. The IT laptop handover is at 11:00. Regional offices follow the same order with local Site Coordinator support.
4. **First 14 days:** complete mandatory training: Information Security Awareness, Code of Conduct and Workplace Safety.
5. **First 30 days:** enroll in benefits (Section 5.6).
6. The probation review takes place at the end of month 3.

### 5.10 Offboarding and Resignation

The standard notice period is 30 days (14 days during probation). The employee submits the resignation through ServiceHub. HR starts the offboarding checklist: IT disables the account at 18:00 on the last day and collects the device within 5 working days (IT-KB-001 Section 6.4); Facilities collects the badge (FAC-KB-001 Section 6.6); Finance requires final expense claims to be submitted by the last working day and the corporate card reconciled (FIN-KB-001 Section 5.8). HR calculates final pay including up to 10 days of unused annual leave.

## 6. Common Scenarios

### 6.1 Leave Request Around a Public Holiday

Public holidays are not deducted from annual leave. If a holiday falls inside the leave period, only the working days are counted. Check the Holiday Calendar for your office's location, because regional offices may differ.

### 6.2 Illness During Annual Leave

If you become ill during approved annual leave and are sick for 3 or more consecutive working days with a medical certificate, notify your manager and HR. Those days are converted to sick leave and returned to your annual leave balance. A sick period of 1 or 2 days during leave is not converted.

### 6.3 Extending a Business Trip with Personal Days

If you add personal days to a business trip, you must request annual leave for those days in ServiceHub before booking. Time away on approved leave is not treated as working time. The company pays only for the business portion of the trip. Costs for personal days are not reimbursable (FIN-KB-001 Section 6.3). Business trips cannot be approved over approved leave unless the manager agrees to change the leave.

### 6.4 Weekend or Holiday Work and Compensatory Days

Obtain manager approval in advance for weekend or holiday work. Record the hours in ServiceHub on the day. The compensatory day is created automatically and must be taken within 60 days. Required weekend business travel is treated as working time under Section 3.5.

### 6.5 Setting Up a Home Workspace

After approval of the standing hybrid arrangement: request the equipment kit from IT, confirm the home address in the HR profile, buy any needed furniture, and claim the USD 300 stipend within 60 days of approval.

### 6.6 Moving to a New City

If the move is for personal reasons and you remain in the same role, update the address (Section 5.5) and submit a Work Arrangement review if you work hybrid. If you transfer to another office, follow Section 5.8. Company-initiated relocations qualify for the allowance; employee-initiated moves do not.

## 7. Exceptions

### 7.1 Leave Beyond 15 Consecutive Working Days

Requests above 15 consecutive working days need approval from the manager, the department head and HR. Approval is usually given for planned extended travel, family reasons or unpaid leave and may require a handover plan.

### 7.2 Exceptional Remote Work

Working from another city in the same country is allowed for up to 10 working days per calendar year with manager approval. Working from another country is allowed for up to 10 working days per calendar year only with approval from the manager and HR, requested at least 15 working days in advance, and after tax and legal review. IT must then approve a Travel Access request (IT-KB-001 Section 6.3). Remote work abroad is not covered by business travel insurance.

### 7.3 Late Benefit Enrollment

Enrollment after the 30-day window is allowed only for a qualifying life event or with HR Director approval for documented reasons. Otherwise the employee waits for the next annual enrollment period.

### 7.4 Contractors

Contractors are not eligible for the entitlements in this document. They are not entitled to the home office stipend or leave. Questions about their working arrangement go to the sponsoring manager.

## 8. Special Cases and Troubleshooting

### 8.1 Leave Balance Looks Incorrect

Check start date, completed service band and pro-rating. Pending requests are included in the balance. If the balance still looks wrong, submit an HR ticket with your Employee ID and the dates concerned. HR replies within 1 working day.

### 8.2 Leave Request Not Decided

If your request has not been decided after 3 working days, the manager is reminded automatically. On the fifth working day it goes to the department head. If travel or deposits are affected, contact HR at ext. 4100.

### 8.3 Profile Update Rejected or Delayed

Rejections are usually caused by missing documents or mismatched names. Read the ticket comments and upload the correct documents. Name, tax and dependent changes take 5 working days. If you have waited longer, raise the HR escalation (Section 9).

### 8.4 Wrong Bank Account or Returned Payment

If a salary or reimbursement payment is returned by the bank, update your bank details (Section 5.5) and submit proof. HR verifies within 3 working days. Payroll re-issues in the next run if the update is done by the 20th. Finance re-issues returned reimbursements in the next weekly payment run (FIN-KB-001 Section 8.5).

## 9. Escalation

1. **HR Service Desk:** first point of contact.
2. **HR Business Partner:** unresolved cases, policy interpretation, manager disputes.
3. **Head of Human Resources:** unresolved or sensitive cases.

For confidential employee relations concerns, email hr-confidential@contoso.example. The Ethics and Conduct Line (Legal & Compliance, ext. 4500) accepts anonymous reports of misconduct. For payroll problems, include "Payroll" in the ticket subject so HR Operations handles it first.
