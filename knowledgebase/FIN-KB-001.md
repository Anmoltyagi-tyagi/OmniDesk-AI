# Finance Knowledge Base — Travel, Expenses and Reimbursement

## 1. Purpose

This document explains how Contoso Enterprise employees book business travel, spend company money, claim reimbursement, use corporate cards, and resolve rejected claims. It states the limits, receipts, approvals and timelines that apply.

## 2. Scope

This document applies to employees who incur business expenses and to the managers who approve them. It covers business travel, hotels, transport, meals, expense claims, receipts, approvals, reimbursement timing, corporate cards, exceptions, rejected claims, and the financial treatment of company assets that are lost or damaged. Leave and work-arrangement rules are in HR-KB-001, technology provisioning in IT-KB-001, and building matters in FAC-KB-001.

## 3. General Policy

### 3.1 Principles

Employees may spend company money only for legitimate business purposes, at reasonable cost, within the limits in this document, and with the approvals required. Expenses must be supported by evidence. Employees may not approve their own expenses or those of someone who reports to them in a circular way. Personal expenses, even if repaid, must not be placed on company cards without prompt repayment (Section 8.4).

### 3.2 Business Travel Pre-Approval

All business travel needs an approved **Travel Request** in ServiceHub (Finance > Travel > New Request) before anything is booked. The request states purpose, dates, destination and estimated cost. The manager approves all requests. A department head must also approve international trips and any trip estimated above USD 2,500. A trip cannot be approved over approved leave unless the leave is changed (HR-KB-001 Section 6.3).

### 3.3 Reimbursable and Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Reimbursable with the required evidence: airfare and rail, hotel within limits, ground transport, meals under per diem, baggage fees for trips of more than 3 nights, visa fees required for business travel, parking and tolls, and pre-approved client hospitality.

Not reimbursable: personal travel days and costs, alcohol (except pre-approved client hospitality), minibar and in-room entertainment, fines and penalties, travel upgrades not approved in advance, spa or personal care services, family members' costs, and personal use of corporate cards.

### 3.4 Currency

Claims are submitted in the currency of the transaction and converted to USD using Finance's monthly exchange rate table for the month of the transaction. For corporate card transactions, the USD amount on the card statement is used.

### 3.5 Submission Deadlines

Submit within 30 days of the end of the trip, or of the expense date for non-travel expenses. Claims 31–60 days late need written manager justification and are flagged. Claims 61–90 days late need Finance Manager approval. Claims more than 90 days late are not reimbursed unless the Finance Controller approves an exception for extraordinary circumstances (Section 7.1). Home Office Setup claims have their own deadline of 60 days from HR approval.

## 4. Eligibility and Applicability

### 4.1 Who May Claim

Employees may claim business expenses. Contractors may claim only expenses allowed by their contract, through their sponsoring manager. Corporate card holders must follow Section 5.8.

### 4.2 Corporate Card Eligibility

Corporate cards are issued to employees in roles that travel or purchase regularly, such as Sales and Operations roles, when the department head and Finance approve the application. The employee must have completed probation. Contractors are not eligible. Default monthly limit is USD 5,000; travel-heavy roles may receive up to USD 15,000 with Finance Manager approval. Cash withdrawals are not permitted except in documented emergencies abroad.

### 4.3 Travel Expense Limits

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Hotel, Tier 1 city (largest metropolitan areas) | USD 220 per night, taxes included |
| Hotel, Tier 2 city | USD 160 per night, taxes included |
| Hotel, Tier 3 or other locations | USD 120 per night, taxes included |
| Meals per diem, domestic | USD 60 per full day (breakfast 12, lunch 18, dinner 30) |
| Meals per diem, international | USD 75 per full day (breakfast 15, lunch 22, dinner 38) |
| Airfare | Economy class; premium economy allowed for flights scheduled over 6 hours with department head approval |
| Rail | Standard class |
| Rental car | Compact class, only with pre-approval |
| Personal vehicle | USD 0.45 per km, with a trip log |
| Client hospitality | Up to USD 75 per person per meal, pre-approved by department head |

The Finance-maintained Travel Desk City Tier List defines Tier 1, 2 and 3 cities. Per diem covers meals without receipts. A partial travel day (under 12 hours away) is paid at 50% of the daily rate. Meals already provided (for example breakfast included in the hotel rate or a hosted dinner) are deducted using the component amounts in this table.

## 5. Procedures

### 5.1 Booking Business Travel

1. Submit and obtain approval of a Travel Request (Section 3.2).
2. Contact the Travel Desk (ServiceHub > Finance > Travel Desk, ext. 4210). Provide travel dates and your approved request number. Book at least 14 days ahead where possible.
3. The Travel Desk books flights, rail and hotel within the limits in Section 4.3. Trips booked through the Travel Desk include company travel insurance and access to the Emergency Travel Assistance line (ext. 4211).
4. Self-booked travel is allowed only with an approved Travel Request and is reimbursed up to the same limits.
5. Changes or cancellations must be reported to the Travel Desk as soon as possible to recover refunds or credits.

### 5.2 Claiming a Hotel Expense

1. Before leaving the hotel, ask for an **itemized folio** showing guest name, check-in and check-out dates, nightly rate, taxes, and a zero balance or proof of payment. A card slip alone is not accepted.
2. In ServiceHub choose Finance > Expenses > New Claim, select the category **Lodging**, enter check-in and check-out dates and the nightly rate, and attach the folio.
3. Enter the Travel Request number as the business purpose reference.
4. If the hotel was booked by the Travel Desk and paid directly by Contoso, no claim is needed for the room. Only incidental charges that are allowed are claimed.
5. Charges not allowed on the folio (minibar, in-room entertainment, spa) must be removed from the claimed amount. If the nightly rate exceeds the limit in Section 4.3, only the limit is paid unless a pre-approval exists (Section 6.2).

### 5.3 Claiming Transport Expenses

Ride-hail and taxi: attach the receipt (an emailed ride-hail receipt is valid). Airport transfers, public transport and rail: attach the receipt or ticket. Rental car: attach the rental agreement, final invoice and fuel receipts. Personal vehicle: attach a trip log showing date, route and kilometers. Parking and tolls need receipts. Select the category **Ground Transport** or **Airfare/Rail** and reference the Travel Request.

### 5.4 Submitting an Expense Claim

1. Open ServiceHub > Finance > Expenses > New Claim.
2. Add each expense line with date, category, amount, currency and business purpose.
3. Attach receipts (Section 5.5). Per diem lines need no receipts.
4. Submit the claim within the deadlines in Section 3.5. The claim is routed automatically for approval (Section 5.6).

### 5.5 Receipt Requirements

A receipt is required for any single expense of USD 25 or more. Receipts must be legible, show the vendor, date, amount, currency and items, and must be itemized for hotels and meals other than per diem. Photos and scans are accepted. Under USD 25, a receipt is not required but the line must be listed with a purpose. For lost receipts, see Section 6.1 and Section 7.2.

### 5.6 Approval Workflow

| Claim total (USD) | Approvers |
|---|---|
| Up to 500 | Direct manager |
| 501 – 2,500 | Manager, then department head |
| 2,501 – 10,000 | Manager, department head, then Finance Manager |
| Above 10,000 | Manager, department head, Finance Manager, then Finance Controller |

Approvers should act within 3 working days. If an approver has not acted by the 5th working day, the claim escalates to the approver's delegate or manager. After approvals, Finance performs a policy review. Employees cannot approve their own claim. The approver listed on your claim is visible in the ServiceHub claim status. If your manager is away, the delegate recorded in the Contoso Directory approves.

### 5.7 Reimbursement Timeline

Typical end-to-end time is 10 working days from submission. Manager approval target is 3 working days per approval level, and Finance review is up to 5 working days. Approved claims are paid in the weekly payment run every Thursday. Claims fully approved by Finance by 17:00 Tuesday are paid that Thursday. Funds normally arrive within 2 working days of the run, in the bank account stored in the HR profile (HR-KB-001 Section 5.5). If a claim is not paid within 15 working days of submission, contact Finance Support.

### 5.8 Corporate Card Use and Reconciliation

1. Apply through ServiceHub > Finance > Corporate Card. Cards are issued within 10 working days of approval.
2. Use the card only for business expenses. Do not use it for personal items.
3. Card transactions are loaded into ServiceHub Expenses within 2 working days. Attach receipts and categorize each transaction within 30 days of the transaction date. The manager approves.
4. Unreconciled transactions older than 45 days lead to card suspension. After 60 days, Finance may recover the amount through payroll deduction after written notice, in line with employment terms and local law, and in coordination with HR.
5. The card must be returned and reconciled by the last working day when leaving the company.

### 5.9 Lost or Stolen Corporate Card

1. Call the card issuer's 24-hour line (on the card back and on the Finance page in ServiceHub) immediately to block the card.
2. Tell Finance Support (ext. 4200 or ServiceHub) within 4 hours.
3. Review recent transactions. Unauthorized charges are disputed with the issuer within 30 days.
4. A replacement is issued within 3 working days. Employees traveling abroad can receive an expedited replacement within 2 working days through the Travel Desk (ext. 4210).

### 5.10 Travel Advances

For international trips of more than 5 nights, employees without a corporate card may request an advance of up to 70% of estimated per diem and non-card expenses, at least 10 working days before departure. The advance must be settled by submitting the expense claim within 15 working days of return. Unsettled advances are recovered from payroll after written notice.

### 5.11 Other Reimbursable Categories

| Category | Rule |
|---|---|
| Home Office Setup | USD 300 one-time, requires an HR approval reference for a standing hybrid arrangement (HR-KB-001 Section 3.4). Claim within 60 days of approval with receipts. Covers furniture and non-IT items only |
| Learning and Development | Up to USD 750 per calendar year, manager pre-approval required. Claim within 30 days of payment |
| Relocation | USD 3,000 lump sum for company-initiated relocations only, with the HR relocation approval reference (HR-KB-001 Section 5.8). Employee-initiated moves are not eligible |
| Software license chargeback | Approved software licenses (IT-KB-001 Section 5.6) are charged to the department cost center; employees do not pay or claim them |
| Catering | Approved catering for meetings is charged to the cost center named in the Facilities request (FAC-KB-001 Section 5.10) |

## 6. Common Scenarios

### 6.1 Lost Receipt

If you lost a receipt, complete a **Missing Receipt Declaration** in ServiceHub with the expense details and attach any substitute evidence (card statement, booking confirmation, ride-hail history).

- Up to USD 100 per expense item: manager approval is enough.
- USD 100.01 to USD 250 per item: Finance Manager approval and supporting evidence required.
- Above USD 250, and hotel stays of any amount: request a duplicate from the vendor first (hotels can usually email a copy of the folio). If a duplicate is not available, the Finance Manager may approve an exception with booking confirmation and proof of payment.

Each employee may use up to 3 declarations per calendar year; a fourth needs Finance Manager approval. Per diem meals never need receipts.

### 6.2 Hotel Rate Above the Limit

If the rate exceeds the Section 4.3 limit, only the limit is reimbursable unless the Travel Request included a documented reason such as a conference block rate and the department head approved the higher rate before booking. A rate above the limit found after the trip needs a Finance Manager exception (Section 7.3) and is not guaranteed.

### 6.3 Personal Days Added to a Business Trip

Only the business portion of the trip is reimbursed. If you stay longer for personal reasons, hotel nights, meals and local transport for the personal days are not reimbursable. If the return fare is higher because of the personal extension, only the fare for the originally planned return date is reimbursed; attach a fare comparison. HR must approve the leave first (HR-KB-001 Section 6.3).

### 6.4 Foreign Currency

Enter the original amount and currency. Finance converts using the monthly rate table. Do not convert manually. Card-paid items use the statement's USD amount.

### 6.5 Cancelled or Changed Trips

Report cancellations to the Travel Desk immediately. Non-refundable costs may be reimbursed if the cancellation was caused by the business. Costs from cancellations for personal reasons are not reimbursed.

### 6.6 Lost or Damaged Company Devices

Lost, stolen or damaged laptops are reported to IT first (IT-KB-001 Sections 5.8 and 5.9). After IT closes the incident, Finance records the loss or damage.

- A lost or stolen laptop is written off at its **net book value**, calculated straight-line over 4 years from purchase.
- The employee is charged only if Information Security finds negligence (for example, leaving the device unattended in a vehicle, or failing to report within 24 hours). The charge cannot exceed net book value.
- Accidental damage: the first incident in 24 months has no charge. A later incident, or one caused by negligence, may be charged up to the lower of the repair cost and net book value.
- The employee receives written notice and may respond within 5 working days. The Finance Manager decides. Any payroll deduction requires written consent or a contractual basis and is coordinated with HR.

### 6.7 Approver Is Unavailable

The delegate recorded in the Contoso Directory may approve. If no delegate is set, the claim escalates to the approver's manager on the 5th working day. Contact Finance Support if you need an earlier decision.

## 7. Exceptions

### 7.1 Late Submission

Submit with a written explanation. Section 3.5 shows the approvals needed. Claims more than 90 days late are exceptional and need Finance Controller approval.

### 7.2 Missing Receipt Exception

Described in Section 6.1. The employee should always attempt to obtain a duplicate first.

### 7.3 Over-Limit Expenses

Exceptions to Section 4.3 require written business justification, department head approval and Finance Manager approval. Pre-approval before the expense is strongly preferred.

### 7.4 Emergencies Abroad

During an emergency (medical event, security incident, lost passport, lost company device), call the Emergency Travel Assistance line (ext. 4211) or the number on the itinerary. Reasonable emergency costs are reimbursable with receipts. Emergency replacement of a lost laptop up to USD 800 is reimbursable under the category **Emergency IT Equipment** if the IT Service Desk Lead pre-approved it; quote the IT ticket number (IT-KB-001 Section 5.9).

## 8. Rejected Claims and Special Cases

### 8.1 Rejection Reason Codes

| Code | Reason | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| R01 | Receipt missing or illegible | Attach a clear receipt, or use a Missing Receipt Declaration (Section 6.1) |
| R02 | Over policy limit | Correct the amount to the limit, or request an exception (Section 7.3) |
| R03 | Submitted late | Provide justification per Section 3.5 |
| R04 | Duplicate claim | Check whether the expense was already claimed or paid on a card |
| R05 | Non-reimbursable category | Remove the item. See Section 3.3 |
| R06 | Missing pre-approval (Travel Request, HR reference, or manager approval) | Obtain the approval and resubmit |
| R07 | Incorrect or incomplete details | Correct category, dates, amount or business purpose |
| R08 | Rejected by approver | Discuss the reason with the approver |

### 8.2 Resubmitting a Rejected Claim

You receive the code and comments by email and in ServiceHub. Correct the claim and resubmit within 10 working days of rejection; the original submission date is kept for deadline purposes. After 10 working days, it is treated as a new claim.

### 8.3 Appealing a Rejection

If you believe a rejection is wrong, submit an appeal in ServiceHub to the Finance Manager within 15 working days of the rejection, including the evidence. A decision is given within 10 working days and is final.

### 8.4 Duplicates and Personal Charges

A duplicate claim for an expense already paid by corporate card is rejected (R04) and may lead to review. Personal charges on a corporate card must be repaid through ServiceHub within 10 working days of discovery. Repeated misuse can lead to card cancellation and action under HR policy.

### 8.5 Payment Problems

If a reimbursement was approved but did not arrive, check the bank account in your HR profile. A wrong or closed account causes the payment to be returned. Update bank details through HR (HR-KB-001 Section 5.5), which takes up to 3 working days. Finance re-issues the payment in the next weekly Thursday run.

## 9. Escalation

1. **Finance Support Desk:** first point of contact.
2. **Expense Team Lead:** claims delayed beyond 15 working days, disputed rejections.
3. **Finance Manager:** exceptions, appeals, high-value claims.
4. **Finance Controller:** claims above USD 10,000, exceptions older than 90 days and unresolved disputes.

Employees traveling and needing urgent help use the Emergency Travel Assistance line (ext. 4211).
