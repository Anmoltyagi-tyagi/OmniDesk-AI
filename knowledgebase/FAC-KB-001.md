# Facilities Knowledge Base — Workplace, Access and Safety

## 1. Purpose

This document describes how Facilities & Workplace Services (Facilities) provides safe, secure and functional offices for Contoso Enterprise. It covers building access, badges, visitors, meeting rooms and desks, maintenance, lost and found, office safety and emergencies.

## 2. Scope

This document applies to everyone who works in or visits the HQ Campus (Buildings A and B), Regional Office East and Regional Office West. It covers physical access, space, building systems and safety. Network, audio-visual equipment and IT devices are handled by IT (IT-KB-001). Leave, work arrangements and accommodations are decided by HR (HR-KB-001). Costs, catering charges and fees are handled by Finance (FIN-KB-001).

## 3. General Policy

### 3.1 Building Hours and Access Times

Buildings are open Monday–Friday 07:00–20:00. The Security desk at Building A reception operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Access outside building hours (evenings, weekends and public holidays) requires an after-hours access flag on the badge (Section 5.3). Employees working alone after hours must sign in with the Security desk (ext. 4999) and sign out when leaving.

### 3.2 Workspace Principles

Hybrid employees use bookable hot desks. Assigned desks are limited to roles designated by the department head. Desks and meeting rooms must be left clean, and personal belongings must be removed at the end of the day from hot desks. Day lockers are free to use; permanent lockers are allocated on request.

### 3.3 Security Principles

Badges are personal and must be visible while in the building. Badges must not be lent. Tailgating, which means following someone through a secured door without badging, is prohibited. Anyone without a badge or visitor badge must be directed to reception. Suspicious behavior is reported to the Security desk.

## 4. Eligibility and Applicability

### 4.1 Access Zones

| Zone | Areas | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Zone A, General | Building entrances, lobbies, common areas, meeting rooms, hot-desk floors | All employees and contractors with a badge |
| Zone B, Department | Floors and areas assigned to a department | Members of the department |
| Zone C, Restricted | Server room, finance records room, HR records room, laboratory | Named individuals approved by the area owner; reviewed every 6 months |

Contractor badges have an expiry matching the contract end date and give access only to the zones in the sponsor's request.

### 4.2 Desk Entitlement

Employees with a hybrid arrangement book hot desks. Employees designated as on-site by the department head have an assigned desk. Employees with medical needs are covered by the ergonomic process (Section 5.8) after HR confirms the accommodation.

### 4.3 Visitor Eligibility

Any employee may host a visitor. Contractors may host only with sponsor approval. A host remains responsible for the visitor throughout the visit. Visitors are not allowed in Zone C. Groups larger than 10 need Facilities approval (Section 7.3).

## 5. Procedures

### 5.1 Obtaining an Access Badge

1. **New employees:** the badge is requested automatically when HR submits the onboarding request (HR-KB-001 Section 5.9), at least 5 working days before the start date.
2. On day 1 at 09:30, collect the badge at the Building A Security desk, bring government photo ID. The photo is taken and the badge is activated with Zone A and the department's Zone B.
3. **Contractors:** the sponsoring manager submits a Contractor Badge request in ServiceHub (Facilities > Access) with the end date of the contract.
4. Regional office employees collect the badge from the local Site Coordinator.

### 5.2 Lost, Damaged or Forgotten Badge

- **Lost or stolen:** report immediately to the Security desk (ext. 4999) or in ServiceHub. The badge is deactivated at once. A temporary badge valid for 3 days is issued the same day after ID verification. The permanent replacement is ready within 2 working days.
- **Replacement fee:** the first replacement in any 12 months is free. Second and later replacements within 12 months cost USD 15, collected by Finance.
- **Damaged badge:** exchange it at the Security desk. A damaged badge returned is free of charge.
- **Forgotten badge:** reception issues a day pass after ID verification. Day passes are limited to 3 per month; further passes need manager confirmation.

If your badge and company laptop were lost together, also report the laptop to IT (IT-KB-001 Section 5.9).

### 5.3 Requesting Restricted-Area or After-Hours Access

1. In ServiceHub choose Facilities > Access > Additional Access.
2. For **Zone C**, state the area, business reason and duration. The area owner and your manager must approve. Facilities activates access within 2 working days.
3. For **after-hours or weekend access**, the manager submits the request with dates. Access is valid for up to 90 days and may be renewed. Weekend working still requires manager approval for compensation under HR-KB-001 Section 3.5.

### 5.4 Registering a Visitor

1. The host registers the visitor in ServiceHub (Facilities > Visitors) at least 1 working day before the visit, giving name, company, date, time and purpose.
2. On arrival, the visitor shows government photo ID at reception. The ID is only inspected and is not retained.
3. Reception notifies the host and issues a visitor badge. The visitor acknowledges the confidentiality notice on the kiosk if entering Zone B.
4. The host or a delegate must escort the visitor at all times beyond the lobby. Visitor hours are 09:00–17:00. Visitor badge printing includes the Contoso-Guest Wi-Fi credentials (IT-KB-001 Section 8.6).
5. On departure, the badge is returned at reception.

Recurring visitors (for example consultants) may receive a temporary badge for up to 30 days when their sponsor requests it.

### 5.5 Booking a Meeting Room

1. In ServiceHub choose Facilities > Rooms and select building, date, time and capacity.
2. Room types: huddle rooms (2–4 people), standard rooms (6–8), large rooms (12–16), and the Boardroom A6-01 (20 seats; department head approval).
3. Rooms can be booked up to 90 days ahead for a maximum of 4 hours per booking. Recurring bookings are allowed for up to 12 weeks.
4. Check in on the room panel within **15 minutes** of the start time. Otherwise the room is released automatically and can be booked by others. Cancel bookings you no longer need.
5. Audio-visual or video-conferencing equipment faults are reported to IT (ext. 4000). Furniture, cleaning and temperature problems are reported to Facilities.

### 5.6 Booking a Desk

Hybrid employees book a hot desk in ServiceHub (Facilities > Desks) up to 5 working days ahead. The booking requires check-in by badge on the floor; a desk not occupied by 10:30 is released. Choose the floor of your department when possible. If no desk is available, contact the Facilities Service Desk and a floor-neutral desk will be offered.

### 5.7 Reporting a Maintenance Issue

1. In ServiceHub choose Facilities > Report an Issue. Select category (Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC, Lighting, Furniture, Cleaning, Pest, Door/Lock, Elevator, Other), enter the exact location (for example, A3 east wing, desk 12), and attach a photo if possible.
2. Choose priority following Section 8.1. If there is danger to people, call ext. 4911 first.
3. Facilities assigns a technician or vendor and updates the ticket, which begins with FAC-. Employees can add comments.
4. Close the ticket when satisfied.

Printer paper and toner requests are submitted as Facilities supply requests; printer faults are IT issues (IT-KB-001 Section 8.9).

### 5.8 Requesting an Ergonomic Assessment

Submit a request in ServiceHub (Facilities > Workplace > Ergonomics). A Facilities ergonomics assessor visits within 10 working days and can recommend a chair, monitor arm or footrest from the standard range. If the need is medical, HR confirms the accommodation first (ticket to HR, ext. 4100); Facilities then provides the equipment and the cost is charged to Facilities.

### 5.9 Lost and Found

1. Items found in the building must be handed to the Security desk or reception within 1 working day.
2. Facilities logs the item in ServiceHub Lost & Found with description, location and date. Employees can search the log.
3. To claim an item, show ID and describe it. The item is released after verification.
4. General items are held for 30 days. Valuables (wallets, phones, keys, ID documents, cash, jewelry) are stored in a secured cabinet and held for 90 days. Unclaimed items are donated or, for valuables, handed to local authorities.
5. **Company property:** found laptops, phones and badges are reported to the IT Service Desk (ext. 4000) and the Security desk. IT verifies the owner and arranges return. Lost badges are deactivated.

Contoso Enterprise is not liable for personal items lost in its offices. Items lost outside Contoso premises are not handled by Facilities.

### 5.10 Requesting Catering or Event Setup

1. When booking the room, select Catering or Event Setup and give attendee numbers and the cost center.
2. Catering requires 3 working days' notice. Events of more than 30 attendees need Facilities approval at least 10 working days in advance (Section 7.3).
3. Catering costs are charged to the named cost center (FIN-KB-001 Section 5.11). Employees do not pay personally.

## 6. Common Scenarios

### 6.1 New Employee First Day

HR submits the onboarding request at least 5 working days before the start date. Facilities then prepares the badge, assigns a locker, and reserves a desk or enrolls the person in hot-desk booking. On day 1 the employee collects the badge at the Security desk at 09:30. If the badge is not ready, reception issues a day pass and Facilities finishes within 1 working day. Laptop collection is at 11:00 at the IT Service Desk.

### 6.2 Moving to Another Office

When HR approves a transfer (HR-KB-001 Section 5.8), Facilities adds the new office's Zone A and department Zone B access, effective 2 working days before the start date, and assigns a desk or locker. The access for the old office is removed on the last day there. Permanent lockers at the old site must be emptied before departure. Personal belongings are not moved by Facilities; moving costs are covered only by an approved relocation allowance (FIN-KB-001 Section 5.11).

### 6.3 Visitor Arrives Unannounced

Reception contacts the host. The visitor waits in the lobby. If the host is available, reception registers the visitor on the spot and the normal escort rules apply. Reception cannot admit anyone whose host is unreachable.

### 6.4 Your Meeting Room Was Taken

If you did not check in within 15 minutes, the room was released (Section 5.5). Find another room in ServiceHub or contact the Service Desk for a free room. If you checked in and someone else is in the room, report it so the booking can be verified.

### 6.5 Company Laptop Found in the Office

If you find a laptop, hand it to the Security desk. Do not power it on or connect it. Security notifies IT, which identifies the owner and arranges return (Section 5.9).

### 6.6 Leaving the Company

Return the badge to the Security desk on the last working day. The badge is deactivated at 18:00 on the last day regardless. Empty permanent lockers. IT collects devices separately (IT-KB-001 Section 6.4).

## 7. Exceptions

### 7.1 Out-of-Hours Access

Access after 20:00 or on weekends without the after-hours flag requires a Security desk escort and a manager's message to Security. Repeated requests should use Section 5.3.

### 7.2 Vendors and Contractors Working On Site

Vendors performing maintenance or installations need a work permit from Facilities and a vendor badge. Work involving hot work, electrical isolation or working at height requires a method statement approved by Facilities. Vendors are escorted in Zone B.

### 7.3 Large Visits and Events

Visits of more than 10 people, and events of more than 30 attendees, need Facilities approval at least 10 working days ahead. Facilities may require additional reception staff, an extended visitor-hours approval, and a floor plan.

### 7.4 Temporary Badge Limits

Temporary badges are limited to 3 consecutive days. After that, a permanent replacement must be collected. Day passes do not grant after-hours access.

## 8. Maintenance Priorities and Special Cases

### 8.1 Priorities and Targets

| Priority | Examples | Response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Flooding, fire alarm failure, gas smell, power failure on a floor, person trapped in an elevator, security breach | 30 minutes (call ext. 4911) | Contained the same day |
| P2 | HVAC failure affecting a floor, toilets out of service, active leak, elevator out of service, broken door lock | 2 working hours | 1 working day |
| P3 | Lighting, furniture repair, minor leak, cleaning request, pest sighting | 1 working day | 5 working days |
| P4 | Cosmetic issues, painting, non-urgent requests | 2 working days | 10 working days |

### 8.2 Electrical Issues

Report sockets that are warm, sparking, cracked or dead. If you smell burning, see sparks or exposed wires, move away, do not touch anything, and call ext. 4911 immediately (P1). Do not reset tripped breakers. Do not use damaged cables.

### 8.3 Plumbing Issues

Report leaks, dripping ceilings, blocked toilets, and taps that do not work. An active leak from a ceiling, near electrical equipment or computers is P2; if water is spreading across a floor it is P1 (call ext. 4911). Move equipment and papers away from the leak if it is safe. Do not attempt repairs.

### 8.4 HVAC and Temperature

The comfort range is 22–24 °C. Thermostat panels are locked and must not be adjusted by employees. Report too hot or too cold areas with exact location and time; Facilities checks within 1 working day. Temperatures below 20 °C or above 26 °C are P2. Portable heaters and fans are not allowed.

### 8.5 Lighting, Elevators and Doors

Lighting faults are P3. An elevator out of service is P2. If you are trapped, press the alarm button and call ext. 4911 (P1). Broken door locks are P2 because they may affect security.

### 8.6 Cleaning and Pest Control

Offices are cleaned every evening and restrooms are checked at midday. Spills and unscheduled cleaning requests are P3. Pest sightings are P3; take a photo if safe and avoid leaving food at desks.

## 9. Office Safety and Emergency Procedures

### 9.1 Fire Alarm and Evacuation

1. Stop work and leave belongings. Do not use elevators.
2. Use the nearest stairs and follow fire wardens in high-visibility vests.
3. Assemble at your assigned point (Building A: Assembly Point 1, front plaza; Building B: Assembly Point 2, Parking Lot B; regional offices: signs at the main entrance).
4. Fire wardens perform roll call. Do not re-enter until the Security desk gives the all-clear.
5. Report anyone who needs help to evacuate to a fire warden. Visitors leave with their hosts.

Evacuation drills are held every quarter at HQ and twice a year in regional offices.

### 9.2 Medical Emergency

Call ext. 4911 and, if life-threatening, the local emergency number. Send someone to reception to guide responders. Trained first aiders (at least one per floor) respond. Automated external defibrillators (AEDs) are at the reception of each building and at the Security desk. The first-aid room is on the ground floor of Building A. Do not move an injured person unless there is immediate danger.

### 9.3 Power Outage, Gas Smell and Severe Weather

- **Power outage:** emergency lighting activates. Stay calm and wait for instructions from fire wardens. Facilities notifies staff through ServiceHub and email.
- **Gas or chemical smell:** evacuate, avoid switches and phones in the area, and call ext. 4911 from outside.
- **Severe weather or building closure:** closures are announced in ServiceHub and by email. Hybrid employees work remotely if they are able to.

### 9.4 Security Threat

Run if there is a safe route, hide if there is not, and tell the Security desk or the local emergency number once safe. Lock doors if you can and silence phones. Do not confront the person. Follow instructions from Security and responders.

### 9.5 Incident Reporting

Report any workplace injury, near miss or safety hazard within 24 hours in ServiceHub (Facilities > Report Incident). If the injury caused absence, notify HR so it is recorded as Work Injury Leave (HR-KB-001 Section 3.2). Facilities keeps the incident report.

### 9.6 Basic Safety Rules

Keep exits and stairwells clear. Do not use personal electrical appliances such as heaters, kettles, toasters and multi-socket extensions. Laptop chargers, monitors and phone chargers are permitted. Do not prop fire doors open. Report spills at once. Do not tamper with fire equipment.

## 10. Escalation

1. **Facilities Service Desk:** first point of contact.
2. **Building or Site Facilities Supervisor:** issues not resolved within the target.
3. **Facilities Manager:** unresolved P1 and P2 issues, vendor disputes.
4. **Head of Facilities & Workplace Services:** serious or recurring issues.

Security concerns go to the Security Supervisor through the Security desk (ext. 4999). Emergencies always go to ext. 4911 and are never queued.
